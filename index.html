Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?
No, swimming is not mandatory for our introductory scuba diving packages. Our certified instructors will guide you throughout the dive.
What is the minimum age?
The minimum age for scuba diving is 10 years. Children must be comfortable in the water.
Is it safe for asthma patients?
Safety is priority. People with asthma, heart conditions, or pregnant women are generally advised not to dive without a doctor's clearance.
