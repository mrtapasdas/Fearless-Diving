Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?
No! For our introductory "Discovery Scuba Dive", swimming skills are not required. An instructor holds you the entire time.
Is it safe for children?
Yes, children above 10 years can do Scuba Diving. Younger children can enjoy Snorkeling.
What should I carry?
Just carry a change of clothes, a towel, and sunscreen. We provide all diving equipment and wetsuits.
