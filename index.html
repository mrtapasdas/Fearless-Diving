Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

No, for the "Try Dive" or "Discovery Scuba" package, swimming is not required. You will have a personal instructor holding you at all times.

Is it safe for children?

Yes, children above the age of 10 can participate in scuba diving. We have special gear for kids.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a change of clothes. We provide all diving equipment.
