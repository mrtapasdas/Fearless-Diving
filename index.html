Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

No, swimming is not required for our beginner scuba diving packages. A personal instructor will be with you at all times.

Is it safe for children?

Yes, children above 10 years old can participate in scuba diving. We have special equipment and trained staff for kids.

What should I carry?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a change of clothes.
