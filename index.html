Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

No, for the "Try Dive" or "Discovery Scuba" package, swimming skills are not required. A personal instructor will hold you at all times.

What is the minimum age?

The minimum age for Scuba Diving is 10 years. For snorkeling, it is 6 years.

Is it safe?

Yes! We have a 100% safety record. We use high-quality gear and every dive is supervised by a certified PADI professional.
