Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

No, swimming is not mandatory for our beginner packages. You will be accompanied by a PADI certified instructor at all times.

Is it safe for children?

Yes, children above 10 years are usually allowed for scuba diving. Snorkeling is safe for younger kids with life jackets.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable swimwear. We provide wetsuits and all necessary diving gear.
