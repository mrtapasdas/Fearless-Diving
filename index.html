Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

For the "Try Scuba" package (Beginners), swimming is NOT required. You will be guided by a personal instructor.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes, children aged 10+ can scuba dive. Younger kids can enjoy snorkeling.

What should I carry?

Just a change of clothes, towel, and sunscreen. We provide all equipment.
