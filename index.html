Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

No, for our "Discover Scuba Diving" package, swimming skills are not required. An instructor will hold you throughout the dive.

What is the minimum age?

The minimum age for scuba diving is 10 years. Children must be comfortable in water.

Is it safe if I have glasses?

Yes! We provide prescription masks if informed in advance, or you can wear contact lenses.

How long does it take?

The entire trip usually takes about 4 to 5 hours, including the boat ride, training, and diving.
