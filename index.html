Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know swimming?

No, for the Discovery Scuba Diving package, swimming is not required. You will be guided by an instructor.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable swimwear. We provide wetsuits, fins, and masks.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes, children above 10 years old can participate in scuba diving with parental consent.
