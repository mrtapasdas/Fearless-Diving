Scuba Diving in Havelock - FAQs

You can book directly through this website by clicking the "Book Now" buttons (which connect to WhatsApp), or visit our dive center at Beach No. 2 (Govind Nagar Beach). We require a small deposit to confirm your slot.

Statistically, driving to the dive shop is more dangerous than diving! We follow strict PADI safety protocols, use well-maintained equipment, and our instructors are certified to handle all situations. We have a 100% safety record.

Snorkeling is done on the surface of the water looking down using a mask and tube. Scuba diving allows you to breathe underwater using a tank and stay submerged for 30-45 minutes to explore the reef up close.
