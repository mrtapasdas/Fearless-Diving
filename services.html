Common Questions

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable swimwear. We provide wetsuits, masks, and fins.

I have glasses, can I dive?

Yes, but you cannot wear glasses inside the mask. We have prescription masks available on request, or you can wear contact lenses (soft lenses recommended).
