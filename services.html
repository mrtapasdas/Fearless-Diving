Statistically, driving to the dive shop is more dangerous than diving! We follow strict PADI safety protocols, use well-maintained equipment, and our instructors are certified to handle all situations. We have a 100% safety record.

Snorkeling is done on the surface of the water looking down using a mask and tube. Scuba diving allows you to breathe underwater using a tank and stay submerged for 30-45 minutes to explore the reef up close.

You must wait at least 12 hours after a single dive, and 18-24 hours after multiple dives before flying. Please plan your trip accordingly to leave one day free before your flight.

It is normal to be nervous! We start in very shallow water where you can stand up. Your instructor will not take you deeper until you feel comfortable. We use hand signals to communicate, and you can end the dive at any time if you feel unwell.

Just bring your swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a change of dry clothes. We provide all diving equipment, wetsuits, and refreshments.

A typical dive trip takes about 1.5 to 3 hours from start to finish. This includes the boat ride, briefing, training, the actual dive (approx. 30 mins), and the return trip.
